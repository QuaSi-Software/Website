As [previously mentioned](https://quasi-software.github.io/Website/news/2026/01/05/start-of-new-project.html) a new project has started this year, which in some ways continues the development from the previous project QuaSi II and adds new open-source tools. You might be wondering about the name Living Loads. While the official project remains called SLP_Ade on the funcing agency's platform, we chose a new name for all other communication, assets and more. We think Living Loads better describes what we are trying to achieve with this project.
