The project team attended the "Jahrestreffen 2026 des Forschungsnetzwerks Energiesystemanalyse" with a short pitch and a poster about the the innovative functionality of the simulation engine ReSiE.

You can view the poster in German online [here]({{'static/260422_poster_esa_meeting_2026.pdf' | absolute_url}}).

<!--more-->

The poster provides an overview of the simulation methodology of ReSiE, the computational engine for the simulation of energy systems. For more details on ReSiE visit the Tools section or check out our [extensive online documentation](https://quasi-software.readthedocs.io/en/latest/).
