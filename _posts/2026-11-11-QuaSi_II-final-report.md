The final report of the QuaSi_II research project has been published and is now freely available online on the website of the German National Library of Science and Technology (TIB).

You can access the final report in German [here](https://doi.org/10.34657/44100).

<!--more-->

The report provides an overview of the project's objectives, the development of the QuaSi simulation software, and the key results achieved during the project. It summarizes the research activities and contributions to the simulation of urban energy systems.

For more information about QuaSi and its functionalities, visit our [online documentation](https://quasi-software.readthedocs.io/en/latest/).
